# Space and shape

## Space

The phone layout is built on one gutter, `--gap`, at 16px. The steps below are for new work, such as the Studio's wider screens. The phone's styles still use raw values inside components, which is fine at this size.

<TokenTable tokens={SPACE_TOKENS} kind="space" />

## Radius

Bigger things get rounder corners. Cards are the roundest thing on a screen, and pills are fully round.

<TokenTable tokens={RADIUS_TOKENS} kind="radius" />

Off the scale on purpose: pixels (3px), the tile strip (2px), the waveform plot (8px), the inner joined tab (9px), and a switch, whose radius is half its height.

## Size

<TokenTable tokens={SIZE_TOKENS} kind="size" />

- Anything you tap is at least `--target` in both directions, even where it looks smaller, such as the Remote and Boots pills.
- The phone layout stops growing at `--phone-column` and centers, so it never stretches on a tablet. The Studio has its own desktop layout instead.
