# Glow and motion

## Glow

Lit things glow, the way an LED does, and unlit things never do. A glow is always the color of the thing that's lit.

## Motion

Motion is feedback, never decoration. Quick things confirm a touch; slow things mirror something the boot is doing, such as the two-second pose capture.

<TokenTable tokens={MOTION_TOKENS} kind="motion" />

- A waiting dot breathes once every `--duration-pulse`. Anything waiting on you, rather than on its own, holds still.
- The pose capture fills its button over `--duration-capture`, the same two seconds the firmware reads, so a hold has a visible end.
- Pattern tile strips run slowly, faster for fire. They stop with everything else.
- All motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
