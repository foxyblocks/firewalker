# Writing

The app talks like a friend who knows the hardware: short, specific, and calm. These rules come from the words already in the app.

## Rules

1. **Name the boot.** "Not saved on Left", not "Save failed".
2. **Say what still works.** "Motion sensor unavailable. Power and brightness still work."
3. **Say what to do next.** "Pause music and keep Left still first."
4. **Plain words, short sentences.** Under about 15 words, in sentence case. No "please", no "successfully", no "error".
5. **Numbers with units, as the boot reports them.** "96 of 96", "9.88 m/s²", "-22.5°".
6. **Buttons say what they do.** Capture, Blink, Reconnect, Calibrate quiet. "Tap again to forget" replaces a confirm dialog.
7. **Reassure about the boots.** When the app loses touch, the boots keep running, so say so: "Connections paused while idle. Your boots keep running."

## Words we use

| Use | Not | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | Shoe, device | It's what you wear. |
| Pattern | Effect, animation, recipe | Recipe is the file format, not what people pick. |
| Strip | LEDs, lights | The physical thing. "Lights" is the power switch. |
| Neutral, heel down, toe down | Calibration points | The poses, as you stand in them. |
| Stomp | Tap, impact | What the firmware detects. |
| Soft cap, Cap | Limit, max | The switch is labeled Cap. |
| Quiet threshold | Noise floor, gate | What you're setting, not how it works. |
