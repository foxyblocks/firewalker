Firewalker — MPU-6050 (GY-521) IMU wiring

Accelerometer/gyroscope variant: an HiLetgo GY-521 (MPU-6050, 6-DOF) read over I2C as the candidate replacement for the FSR stomp sensor. Used by the imu_debug firmware mode. The IMU adds four I2C wires; the NeoPixel strip, D6 debug button, and power wiring are unchanged from docs/wiring.md.

3.3 V (IMU power) SDA (I2C data) SCL (I2C clock) Strip data Debug button +5 V (strip) GND (common)
Why 3.3 V, not 5 V: the GY-521 has an onboard regulator and I2C pull-up resistors. Powering VCC from the Feather's 3V3 pin pulls SDA/SCL up to 3.3 V, matching the Feather's logic level — so the I2C bus needs no level shifter. (The 5 V NeoPixel data line is a separate story; see the extra-effort reference.)

Key rules

  • Power the GY-521 from 3V3, not USB/5 V — keeps SDA/SCL at the Feather's logic level (no level shifter on the I2C bus).
  • Common ground everywhere — Feather, IMU, and the strip's 5 V supply share one GND.
  • AD0 sets the address. GND → 0x68 (matches IMU_I2C_ADDR); 3V3 → 0x69.
  • The IMU is additive. Strip power/data and the debug button are exactly as in docs/wiring.md; in imu_debug the FSR/A0 input is simply unused.
  • The strip is still 5 V and still wants the level-shifter/resistor/cap treatment for a robust build — see docs/wiring-extra-effort.html.

Firmware: pio run -e imu_debug -t upload, then pio device monitor -e imu_debug (115200 baud). Thresholds live in include/config.h and are starting points to tune on a real foot.
