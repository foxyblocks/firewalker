Firewalker — Feather RP2040 extra-effort wiring

Reference wiring for a more protective build: Adafruit Feather RP2040 (3.3 V) driving a 5 V NeoPixel strip through a level shifter and data resistor, with an A0 FSR "stomp" sensor and D6 debug button. The current minimal rig in docs/wiring.md omits the level-shifter extras because it is working on the bench.

+5 V (red) GND (common) Data — 3.3 V in, 5 V out of shifter FSR sense 5 V test option (Feather USB pin)
Why the level shifter: the Feather is 3.3 V; WS2812 NeoPixels at 5 V want a data HIGH near 3.5 V, so a bare 3.3 V line is marginal. A 74AHCT125 (powered from 5 V, OE→GND) translates the data to a clean 5 V. Short bench runs sometimes work without it — add it the moment the strip looks glitchy.

Key rules

  • Common ground everywhere — Feather, shifter, 5 V source, and strip grounds all tied. The data signal is meaningless without a shared reference.
  • Strip power never flows through the Feather. The Feather only drives the (level-shifted) data line. Its 3.3 V regulator can't run the strip.
  • 5 V for the strip: the Feather USB pin (VBUS) for a quick test, or a dedicated 5 V source (LiPo + 5 V boost, or a power bank) for real use. Never the 3V3 pin.
  • Protection: 300–470 Ω in series on the data line near the strip; ~1000 µF across the strip's 5 V↔GND.
  • Power budget: up to ~2 A per 60-LED meter at full white. Keep brightness and lit length sane on battery — see docs/power-budget.md.

Use this as a backup reference when the minimal wiring in docs/wiring.md becomes flaky, brighter, longer, or wearable.
