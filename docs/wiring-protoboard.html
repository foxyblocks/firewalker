Firewalker — FeatherWing Proto carrier layout

Physical placement for FOX-183: the FeatherWing Proto carries the GY-521 IMU, the D6 button, and a JST pigtail to the strip. The Feather plugs into female sockets on the wing's top face; the whole assembly mounts flipped, so the wing's underside is the outward face. Nets are unchanged from docs/wiring.md + docs/wiring-mpu6050.md.

With USB up: 12-pin row (BAT…SDA) on the left, 16-pin row (RST…D4) on the right. Confirm each hole against the wing's silkscreen + a continuity check before soldering.

Routing is indicative, nets are normative. Wire paths above are schematic — run them through whatever free grid holes are convenient (including under the GY-521 body, which stands off on pin stubs). The inner duplicated row next to each header pin is the intended solder point for jumpers; the bused 3V3/GND strips are convenient for power after a continuity check confirms which is which on this face.

Decisions baked into this layout (FOX-183)

  • Inner ankle, both shoes, identical builds. Left vs right is a firmware foot-side flag (final_app_left / final_app_right) flipping the chirality-sensitive IMU signs — the hardware is not mirrored.
  • Strip DIN starts directly under the assembly (pixel 0 at the inner ankle; the strip is a 40-px ring — toe at 13, heel at 33), so the JST pigtail run is short.
  • Protoboard beneath the Feather, female stacking sockets on the wing's top face; assembly mounts flipped so the wing underside faces outward.
  • USB points up the leg; JST pigtail exits the down edge toward the shoe.
  • GY-521 + D6 button live on the exposed face — button pressable, IMU clear of the under-Feather gap.
  • No data-line series resistor, no level shifter — direct 3.3 V data is confirmed working on this rig.
  • Per-foot IMU sign confirmation after assembly (imu_debug pass on each shoe): axis assignments match the bench-confirmed USB-up orientation, but which foot gets which sign of IMU_MOUNT_FOOT_SIGN is a hypothesis until measured.

Why the long 5 V run

The USB/VBUS pin sits at the up-leg end of the 12-pin row while the JST exits the down edge, so the strip's 5 V feed runs nearly the full board length. At the ~600 mA worst case (full-strip flash) that's fine over real hookup wire — don't rely on a thin wisp of solder bridging grid holes.
